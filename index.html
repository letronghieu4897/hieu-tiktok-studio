hieu-tiktok-studio

hieu-tiktok-studio is a small personal tool that helps the creator of the TikTok channel Khoa Học Cuộc Sống (@khoa.hoc.cuoc.song) upload and publish their own videos to TikTok using the TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. The channel owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  2. The owner selects a video file prepared by themselves.
  3. The app uploads the video to the owner's own TikTok account, as a draft or a post, using the Content Posting API.

The app only accesses accounts whose owners have explicitly granted permission. It does not collect data from other users.
