Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. What we collect

When you sign in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and your TikTok open ID, and, if you grant the permission, basic profile information such as display name. The app also handles the video files you choose to upload.

2. How we use it

This information is used only to upload and publish the videos you select to your own TikTok account. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored only as long as needed to provide the service and are deleted when you revoke access or ask us to delete them. Video files are not kept after the upload finishes.

4. Your choices

You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. You can also contact us to request deletion of any data we hold about you.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect tokens and data, but no system is completely secure.

6. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

7. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/letronghieu4897/hieu-tiktok-studio/issues.
